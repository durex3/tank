## 实际提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE polished production design reference render of a modular four-wheeled toy tank for a mobile game. Full vehicle isolated on plain dark muted purple background, three-quarter front view facing lower left, mildly elevated orthographic-like camera, no UI or lettering. Art direction locked: charming compact Q-version light mechanical style, clean rounded kart-like body, generous soft bevels and broad smooth armor surfaces, simple readable silhouette with a few purposeful mechanical details. Low wide chassis, exactly four oversized identical round charcoal rubber tires (two on each side), restrained large tread blocks, flat porcelain-white wheel discs with warm yellow rim rings and a small black horizontal slot. Four simple smooth white fenders. White low sloping front hood, two tiny cyan headlights, charcoal underbody. Compact squat white armored single-cannon turret, approximately 62 percent of total chassis width; turret housing height about half a tire diameter. Turret sits directly on the chassis on a THIN charcoal rotation bearing, NO visible neck, NO raised white cylinder or pedestal, NO gap between chassis and turret. Thick straight charcoal cannon barrel of moderate length with a warm yellow muzzle ring and dark recessed bore, pointing forward aligned with vehicle. Turret housing is rounded rectangular, not spherical, with two subtle panel breaks and three small dark vent slots. Restrained neutral porcelain-white 65 percent, charcoal 30 percent, warm yellow 5 percent, tiny cyan indicators only. Matte painted toy metal and rubber, no photorealistic texture noise. Soft neutral studio lighting with gentle contact shadows and readable volumes, precise manufactured shapes, high quality in-game asset aesthetic suitable for reproducible Blender modeling. Entire silhouette in frame with margin, large central subject. Exclude damage, dents, crumpled surfaces, faceted broken armor, scratches, grime, dense seams, tiny screws, cables, external reservoirs, backpacks, human characters, tracks, extra wheels, giant turret, tall turret mount, scenery, display pedestal, logos, labels, multi-view collage. This is the complete assembled vehicle design reference, not a 3D model deliverable.
